Nine buttons to log a meal. One tap is enough. The American food list is built in. No measuring, no deciding. Free for 7 days, then a paid plan.

> **Tip:** Do not repeat words already in the app name or subtitle — Apple's algorithm already indexes those. Rotate seasonal variants (e.g. swap "fitness" for "new year goal") without a new build.

### 2-5. Description (4000 chars max)

```
The biggest enemy of meal tracking is trying to track it perfectly.

Hachibu is built around the opposite idea: roughly is enough.

A subscription (monthly or annual) is required. The first 7 days are free, and you won't be charged if you cancel during the trial.

■ Nine buttons, one tap (Quick Log)
Tap a category button — kcal and macros logged instantly. Ingredient tab or dish tab, nine buttons each. Your most-logged foods rise to the top automatically. No measuring. No deciding.

■ Foods you actually eat
Choose the American food list and the buttons, dishes and portion sizes follow: burgers, burritos, pizza by the slice, wings, bowls, coffee-shop drinks, and chain-restaurant orders. Search is there when you want it — type it your way, like "glass of milk" or "two slices of pizza".

■ More time? Hold for detail
Long-press the same button to add type, cooking method, and amount. Log as much or as little as you feel like — the app doesn't care either way.

■ A goal that fits
Enter your stats, pick a direction. Get a daily calorie and macro target — realistic pace, not a crash diet.

■ A quiet weekly look back
Once a week, a short recap: calories against your target, the balance of protein, fat and carbs, and what you ate most. No score, no grade.

■ Take your data with you
Export your log as a CSV and share it anywhere — email, Files, or an AI app — to review it your own way.

■ Works with Apple Health
Optionally read steps, active energy and workouts to adjust your daily target. Read-only, and you can turn it off any time.

■ No streaks, no score
Miss a day — the app won't mention it.

■ Pricing
Free for the first 7 days. After that, your chosen plan renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in your store's subscription settings. Prices are shown in the store.

■ Privacy
Your meals and body data stay on your device. No account needed. The app sends only anonymous usage counts to help improve it, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Hachibu (八分) — the Japanese practice of stopping at 80% full. Roughly is enough.
```

*(~145 words)*
