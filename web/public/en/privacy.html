Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · 日本語

Hachibu (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is managed.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 User-Entered Data

The App stores the following information only in your device's local storage when you enter it:

  • Height, weight, age, and biological sex
  • Meal logs (foods, dishes, amounts, timestamps)
  • Weight and body fat percentage records
  • Settings (goals, activity level, etc.)

This information is never sent to third-party servers.

1.2 Subscription Information

We use RevenueCat to manage paid plans. RevenueCat collects the following to verify and manage purchases:

  • An anonymized user identifier
  • Purchase history
  • Device platform (iOS / Android)

See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

1.3 Payment Information

All purchases are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The App does not access your credit card information.

1.4 Health Data (Apple Health / Health Connect)

Only if you grant permission, the App reads the following data:

  • Steps
  • Active energy (calories burned)
  • Workouts
  • Weight and body fat percentage

This data is used on your device only, to adjust your daily calorie target and display your records. Health data is never sent to or stored on external servers or iCloud, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with third parties. The App does not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can revoke permission at any time in your device settings.

1.5 Camera and Barcodes

The camera is used only to scan barcodes. Camera images are never saved or sent. The scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, an external food database, to look up nutrition information.

1.6 Crash and Error Reports

To find and fix problems, the App uses Sentry to send crash and error diagnostics (such as device model, OS and app version, and the error details and location). This information is never used for advertising or tracking.

1.7 Usage (anonymous)

To improve the App, it uses PostHog to send anonymous usage information, such as which screens you open and whether actions like logging or purchasing happened. Only categories are sent: screen and action types, app version, device type, language, and days since install. What you ate, numbers such as weight, body fat, height and age, search words, and typed text are never sent. The App does not use your name, email, or advertising ID (IDFA), and IP addresses are not stored. You can turn this off anytime in Settings > Usage.

1.8 Data Export (only when you choose)

Only when you tap "Export data" (in Settings, or at the end of the weekly recap), the App turns your records, such as meals, weight, and exercise, into a CSV file and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example email, Files, or an AI app. Once shared, the data is handled under the policy of the app you chose. The App never sends it by itself, and your profile and settings are not included.

2. Third-Party Sharing

The App does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Deletion

Deleting the App removes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of purchase information held by RevenueCat, please contact contact@akizony.com.

4. Cookies / Tracking

The App does not use cookies or IDFA for advertising or tracking. The usage information in 1.7 is never used for advertising or tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

6. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice. Material changes will be announced within the App.

7. Contact

For inquiries about this policy, please contact contact@akizony.com.
